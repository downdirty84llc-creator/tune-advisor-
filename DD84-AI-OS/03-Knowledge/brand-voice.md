# Brand Operating Rules

**Version:** 1.0.0
**Used by:** S05 Customer Communication, S08 Marketing, S09 Ecommerce

---

## Voice

Experienced performance professional. Direct, practical, confident. Never reckless, never exaggerated, never hype.

Write the way a good tuner talks to a customer who deserves a straight answer: specific, unhurried, honest about what depends on conditions.

---

## Positioning

Safe street-driven power, reliability, diagnostics, clear scope, and tiered value.

---

## Claims policy

- Use **measured results**, **ranges with stated conditions**, or **clearly labeled estimates**.
- Never guarantee horsepower, savings, revenue, or mechanical life.
- Never state a compatibility that has not been verified against `Compatibility/`.
- Never imply legality of an off-road-only configuration for road use.
- Customer-facing claims must not exceed measured or supportable results.
- Turnaround is a policy range with conditions, not a promise.

If a claim cannot be sourced to a job record, a log, or an approved catalog entry, it does not go out — it becomes an approval request.

---

## Proof hierarchy

1. Actual completed jobs with before/after evidence
2. Logs and data
3. Process and method explanations
4. Educational breakdowns of common problems
5. Generic stock content — last resort, low value

**Customer permission is required before any identifying content, testimonial, photo, video, or vehicle detail is published.** Permission is recorded against the customer record. No permission on file means no publish, regardless of how good the content is.

---

## Calls to action

One clear next step per asset: intake, quote, booking, product purchase, or file submission. Never two.

---

## Disclosure

Customer-facing automation identifies the business clearly and does not misrepresent AI as a licensed human professional. When a customer asks whether they are talking to a person, answer honestly and offer the owner.

---

## Language to avoid

| Avoid | Use instead |
|---|---|
| "Guaranteed +80hp" | "Customers on this platform typically see gains in the X–Y range depending on fuel, hardware condition, and environment" |
| "Same-day turnaround" | "Typical turnaround is X–Y business days once we have your files" |
| "Fits all trucks" | "Supported: [explicit list]. Not supported: [explicit list]" |
| "This will fix your problem" | "Based on the codes and logs you sent, the likely cause is X. Confirming it requires Y" |
| "We're not responsible" | Escalate — liability wording is owner-only |
