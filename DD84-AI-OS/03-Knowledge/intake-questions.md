# Required Intake Questions

**Version:** 1.0.0
**Used by:** S02 Lead Intake, S03 Quote Builder, S06 Service Delivery

The goal is a complete opportunity record with the **fewest questions asked**. Extract everything already present in the customer's message before asking anything. Ask only what materially affects fit, safety, scope, price, or scheduling.

---

## The required set

**Vehicle and condition**
- Year, make, model, engine, transmission
- Current operating condition — running, driveable, known faults
- Complete modification list
- Fuel type / octane actually used
- Tire size, gear ratio, converter or clutch details when relevant

**Software and history**
- Existing tune / software state, and who did it
- Diagnostic trouble codes present
- Recent repairs
- Available logs and files

**Outcome**
- Desired outcome: reliability, driveability, power, towing, track, fuel change, feature change, or diagnosis
- What problem prompted the inquiry

**Logistics**
- Customer location
- Mobile / in-shop / remote preference
- Timing
- Budget readiness

**Acknowledgment**
- Customer acknowledges that actual power and outcome depend on hardware condition, environment, fuel, and test data

---

## Asking rules

- **Batch, don't drip.** One message with the missing items, not five sequential questions.
- When the missing set is large, send the structured intake link instead of asking inline.
- Never ask for something already in the record or already stated in the thread.
- Never ask a question whose answer would not change the quote, the schedule, or the safety assessment.
- Restricted data (full VIN, payment details) is requested only through a secure path, never in an open message thread.

---

## Screening — stop and escalate

Raise to S07 / owner rather than continuing intake when the inquiry involves:

- An unsupported platform or controller
- An unsafe current condition — brakes, steering, fire risk, unstable electrical
- An illegal request: emissions defeat for road use, VIN or odometer misrepresentation, immobilizer bypass
- Unclear mechanical condition that makes any scope estimate unsound
- A customer expectation the business cannot support (guaranteed power figure, guaranteed timeline)

---

## What intake may never promise

Power output, a specific outcome, an appointment before eligibility is confirmed, a final price outside the price book, a turnaround guarantee, or compatibility that has not been verified against `Compatibility/`.
