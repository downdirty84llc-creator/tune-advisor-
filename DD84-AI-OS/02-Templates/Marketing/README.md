# Marketing Templates

**Used by:** S08 Marketing and Growth, S09 Website and Ecommerce
**Governed by:** `03-Knowledge/brand-voice.md`

---

## Pre-publish checklist — every asset, every time

- [ ] **Claim check** — every claim traced to a job record, log, or approved catalog entry
- [ ] **Price check** — any price shown matches the current price book version
- [ ] **Compatibility check** — any platform claim matches the register, with exclusions stated
- [ ] **Permission check** — customer likeness, vehicle detail, or testimonial has recorded permission
- [ ] **Privacy check** — no private customer data, no identifying detail without permission
- [ ] **Guarantee check** — no guaranteed power, revenue, savings, mechanical life, legality, or results
- [ ] **CTA check** — exactly one call to action
- [ ] **Attribution** — campaign ID and tracking links attached

Any unchecked box → approval packet, not a publish.

## Campaign brief

```
CAMPAIGN {campaign_id}

Objective:        {one revenue objective}
Audience:         {one segment}
Problem:          {one customer problem}
Offer:            {one approved offer}
Proof:            {one proof point — job ID or log}
Call to action:   {one next step}

Channels:         {list}
Budget:           ${amount}
Baseline:         {current metric value}
Success metric:   {the single metric this moves}
Review dates:     7 days: {date}   30 days: {date}
Decision rule:    keep if {threshold} / revise if {threshold} / stop if {threshold}
```

---

## Content derivation

One long-form source asset → short video script · caption · email · website FAQ entry · sales reply.

**Facts do not change between formats.** If a claim is too qualified to fit a caption, the caption is wrong — not the qualification.

---

## Proof post — permission gate

```
Job:                 {job_id}
Permission on file:  [ ] yes  [ ] no
Permission scope:    [ ] photos  [ ] video  [ ] results  [ ] name  [ ] vehicle detail
Recorded:            {date, against customer record}
```

**No permission record → no publish.** Not "customer seemed fine with it." A record.

## Referral outreach

```
Segment:              {repair shop | trans builder | exhaust | parts | dealer | fleet | towing | detailer | events}
Specific value to them: {what this segment actually gains — not a generic partnership pitch}
What we ask for:      {one thing}
Referral value:       {fee or reciprocal value}
Compliance notes:     {constraints}
Follow-up:            {date}
```

Monthly to active partners: proof of responsiveness and outcomes — **without protected customer information.**
