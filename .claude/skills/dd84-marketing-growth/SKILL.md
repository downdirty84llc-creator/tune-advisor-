# S08 — Marketing and Growth

Create a measurable pipeline using approved offers and brand voice.

## Read before acting

- `DD84-AI-OS/03-Knowledge/brand-voice.md` — voice, claims policy, proof hierarchy, permission rules
- `DD84-AI-OS/03-Knowledge/Price-Book/README.md` — the only source of any published price
- `DD84-AI-OS/02-Templates/Marketing/` — approved templates

## Rules

- Start with **one** audience, **one** problem, **one** approved offer, **one** proof point, **one** call to action.
- Repurpose source material into channel-appropriate content **without changing facts**.
- Use only approved prices, compatibility, turnaround, service scope, and performance claims.
- Never guarantee power, revenue, savings, mechanical life, legality, or results.
- Tag every asset and lead with a `campaign_id` for attribution.
- Recommend tests based on conversion data, not vanity metrics.

## Approval required before publishing

- Customer likeness, identifying vehicle information, or testimonials
- Any performance claim or measured result
- A new promotion, offer, or discount
- Any price change
- Controversial or legally sensitive topics
- Any new public policy statement

**Permission check is mandatory** before any customer content: permission on file, recorded against the customer record. No record, no publish — no matter how good the content is.

## Weekly campaign engine

| Day | Deliverable | Approval rule |
|---|---|---|
| **Monday** | Offer and target audience brief; pipeline gap analysis | New offer requires approval |
| **Tuesday** | Educational post/reel script from a common customer problem | Pre-approved factual templates may publish |
| **Wednesday** | Proof post from completed work | Customer likeness/results require approval |
| **Thursday** | Referral/partner outreach batch and follow-up tasks | Approved partner scripts autonomous |
| **Friday** | Product/service spotlight, FAQ, booking CTA | Approved pricing only |
| **Saturday** | Behind-the-scenes or project content | No private customer data |
| **Sunday** | Performance report and next-week test recommendation | Internal only |

## Content production workflow

1. Select one revenue objective and one target segment.
2. Choose one approved offer, proof point, or educational problem.
3. Create a long-form source asset, then derive: short video script, caption, email, website FAQ, sales reply.
4. Run claim, privacy, permission, and price checks.
5. Publish or request approval based on content class.
6. Attach campaign IDs and tracking links so leads and revenue can be attributed.
7. At 7 and 30 days, compare against baseline and recommend **keep, revise, or stop**.

## Referral partner workflow

- Segment the list: repair shops, transmission builders, exhaust shops, performance parts sellers, dealerships, fleets, towing, detailers, event organizers.
- Write a **specific** value proposition per segment. A generic partnership pitch converts nothing.
- Log: contact, response, agreed process, referral fee or reciprocal value, compliance constraints, next follow-up.
- Send monthly proof of responsiveness and outcomes to active partners — **without sharing protected customer information**.
- Measure partner-sourced leads, conversion, revenue, margin, and repeat rate.

## Success measures

- Lead cost and conversion tracked per campaign
- Content cadence achieved
- Referral follow-up completion above **95%**
