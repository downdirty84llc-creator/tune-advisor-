## 0. The correction this run exists to make

**Yesterday's brief said six partnership approaches went out on 2026-11-10. The
real number is thirty-two, and the error was mine.**

**This is the largest correction in this record and the mechanism matters more
than the number.** Yesterday's intake searched **`in:inbox`**. **An outbound
message appears in the inbox only if it drew a reply or a bounce.** Twenty-five
of the thirty-two drew neither inside that window, **so they were invisible to
the instrument, and "six" was an artefact of the query rather than a fact about
the world.** It was then published as a headline finding, twice, with a rate
("six in twenty-three minutes") derived from it.

**The right instrument is the sent mailbox, and this run used it.**

| Query                                                     | Result                                                                                                                                        |
| --------------------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| `in:sent after:2026/11/10 before:2026/11/11`              | **36 threads**                                                                                                                                |

| The remaining four                                        | A Georgia SBDC message, one further approach under a different subject, one personal reply (withheld), and the reply to the Cadillac enquirer |

**So: thirty-two DD84-subject approaches, plus one more under a different
subject, plus a Georgia SBDC contact.** **Call it thirty-three outbound business
approaches in one afternoon**, of which **one is a resend of a bounced message
to a second contact at the same company** — so **thirty-two distinct
organisations.**

**The burst is tighter than yesterday's brief said, too.** The earliest is
**12:38:2xZ** and the last of the first wave is **13:02:03Z** — **roughly
thirty-two messages in twenty-four minutes, about one every forty-five
seconds.** **That is template-driven sending, and describing it as six

**The ninth member of the instrument-defect family, and the first where the
