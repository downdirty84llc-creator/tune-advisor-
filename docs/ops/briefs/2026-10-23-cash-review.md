pattern.**

**The intake's 24-hour window could not see it.** That is the same instrument

it for the phenomenon. **The weekly review exists precisely to catch this**, and
this is the first week it has.

repeatedly for a week while payments failed across five providers. **That is
consistent with someone controlling which charges land, which is a
cash-management behaviour rather than a fault.** It is not asserted as intent —
**but T-71's framing of "two payments declined" is now third-order wrong**:
there were more than two, across more than one instrument, over more than one
day.
